# UX review — 26 Oct 2026 (round 5)

Scope: everything added since round 4 — the Group activities planner, group session + check-in, the two new
cards (resident profile "Group activities", home-admin "Group programme") and the Care at home flow.
Method: full walk on **iPad Air 11** (primary target — decisions below are iPad-first), a short pass at phone
width (secondary, noted only), code read of `GroupActivityPlanner`, `GroupActivityPlannerScreens`,
`GroupSessionScreens`, `HouseholdScreens`, `HouseholdCareFlow`, and one Dynamic Type check at
*accessibility-medium*. One bug found during the review was fixed on the spot (see B0).

| Brief | Status | What is still open |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Intelligent resident recommendations** — limited one-to-one / few activities / isolation risk / interests aligned with upcoming activities; proactive alerts | **Covered**: need score (overdue ≥7 days, no group in 14 days, declining/unsettled), "Overdue a visit" / "Due a group" flags, the *nobody overlooked* nudge, planner chips | • "Upcoming" activities do not exist — the planner is *now only*; there is no plan/schedule to match residents against. • Isolation risk is inferred from this app's sessions only; no attendance signal (invited ≠ joined), no declined invitations. • Alerts live inside the planner; the roster and dashboard do not surface "3 residents have had nothing for a week" unless staff open it |
| **2. Activity matching on preferences** — rock / classical / mixed groups | **Covered**: per-resident genre affinity → one activity per genre with ≥3 fans, "Shared favourites" mixed set | • **The chosen theme does not shape the playlist** (`GroupSessionPlaylistCompiler` ranks by the participants' overall tastes). | **3. AI-powered staff suggestions** — who benefits most / who shares tastes / who lacks attention / which activities / who needs one-to-one | **Covered**: attention, organise, pairing (×2), one-to-one (×2) nudges, each with Add | • Nudges capped at 2 per kind; profile-set "prefers one-to-one" residents lose their slot to heuristic "unsettled" ones. • The *organise* nudge duplicates the top activity card when nobody is overdue. • "Who benefits most" is a sort order the list does not make visible (B4) |
| **4. Wellbeing & engagement tracking** — participation levels, regulars, needs encouragement, best activities | **Covered at session level**: 4 group ratings at check-in, engagement overview on the planner, participation line on the profile, "Group programme" card for Home Admin | • **No per-resident engagement inside a group** — a session records one engagement score for the room; the profile line and "activities that land best" inherit it. • Attendance = the invited list; "left early / did not join" is not recordable. • A skipped check-in still counts as full participation for everyone (B1) |
| **5. Staff control & personalised care** — suggestions only; consider cognitive abilities, accessibility, emotional wellbeing, care needs | **Half covered**: every suggestion is optional; staff pick; `groupComfort` (enjoys / small groups / one-to-one) is staff-set on the profile; "keep it gentle" from trend/unsettled sessions; `dislikes` text ("chatter", "crowd") → small groups | • **Cognitive ability, accessibility requirements and care needs are not modelled anywhere** — no fields on `CarePatientProfile`, nothing the planner can read. Implied by the brief but not asked for explicitly: scheduling (date/time, recurring activities), room capacity and
staff ratio, invitations that can be declined. None exist; the planner starts a session immediately.

## B. Findings — iPad first, by severity

### B0. Fixed during the review
- **Group history leaked across homes.** Riverside House's "Group programme" card showed *"Lands best: Soul session"*
  from Maple Lodge because group records are one store and the planner did not scope them. `GroupActivityPlanner.build`
  now keeps only records that involved the residents it is given. Built clean; verified on Riverside.

### High — behaviour / product
1. **A skipped whole-home session counts as a group for everyone.** A 38-second whole-home playlist, check-in skipped,
   wrote a record with all 31 participants: the planner then read *"0 due a group"*, every resident became *Regular*,
   and the dashboard's "would welcome a group" line emptied. Recommend: a minimum to count as participation
   (≥ 5 min or ≥ 2 tracks), whole-home records never satisfy "due a group" (or weight 0.5), and "Skip" on a group
   check-in under a minute asks *Discard this session?* instead of saving it.
2. **The theme does not shape the playlist** (A2). Pass the plan's theme into the compiler (theme genre first, weight
   ×3); production needs several tracks per genre — today a session is at most 4 unique titles.
3. **Start is buried.** On iPad the primary action sits under 31 resident rows and the engagement card; staff scroll
   three screens to start. Recommend a sticky bottom bar (*Start group session (8)* + *8 selected*) and name chips of
   who is selected beside it, so the final list can be checked without scrolling back.
4. **Resident order reads as arbitrary.** "Sorted by who would benefit most" collapses to near-random once scores tie,
   and a resident the planner says should be seen one-to-one (Frank J.) can top the list. Recommend sections:
   *Would benefit most* · *Seen recently* · *Better one-to-one today* (collapsed at the bottom), alphabetical inside each.
5. **One-to-one nudges contradict the rows.** "Consider a one-to-one for Frank J." when his row says *one-to-one
   1 day ago*; profile-set *Prefers 1-to-1* residents (Ethel W., Gladys M.) were cut by the cap of 2. Recommend: profile
   preference ranks first; phrase by recency ("saw them yesterday — keep it one-to-one for now" vs "plan a visit");
   one combined line instead of a hard cap.
6. **A home lead cannot reach the roster or the planner.** Alex's dashboard offers only *Switch care home*; Riverside
   House has no supervisor account, so its roster and planner are unreachable in the demo. Recommend an *Open roster*
   action on the dashboard for home leads, and a Riverside supervisor demo account.

### Medium — clarity / consistency
7. **Trend strip clips the third chart on iPad** (admin dashboard and household insights) although the column is
   ~930 px wide. Size the compact cards to fit three across in the regular width class; keep the horizontal scroll for
   compact width only.
8. **Redundant nudge.** "8 residents respond to jazz — a jazz lounge is the easiest win today" repeats the activity card
   directly above it when nobody is overdue. Show the *organise* nudge only when it adds information.
9. **Zero-state chips.** "0 overdue a visit · 0 due a group" are noise; hide zero chips or say *Nobody overdue*.
10. **Copy.** *fortnight* (planner status lines, engagement card, dashboard card) vs *last 14 days* everywhere else —
    standardise on "14 days". *"Latest day: 1 sessions"* in trend cards. Dot-ellipsis *"Leslie X...."* in the planner's
    engagement card (two places; the dashboard card already uses "and N more"). Household line *"Cared for at home ·
    family member Claire"* → *"Claire · family member"*. Group session hint *"compiled from resident listening data
    across your home"* is wrong for a planned activity (it is compiled from the chosen residents).
11. **Group session chrome is the odd one out**: a custom *End session / Log out* bar, cards edge-to-edge instead of the
    centred column, no title. Use the shared layout width; put the activity ("Jazz lounge · 5 residents") in the title
    slot rather than a muted line.
12. **Group check-in has no per-resident step** (A4). An optional last step — *Who stood out?* with resident chips
    (engaged · unsettled · left early) — would give the profile line and "activities that land best" real per-person data.
13. **Demo jump `group` bypasses the planner** (`performDemoJump` still calls `beginGroupSession()`; the demo README
    describes the planner). Point it at `beginGroupActivityPlanning()`.
14. *Pine Smoke Drift (1)* appears in group playlists — the known interim gospel stem.

### Low — polish, accessibility, phone width
15. **Dynamic Type.** At accessibility sizes every text scales except segmented pickers (*In company*, roster display
    mode, *I am a*, share kind), which stay tiny. Use a vertical option list when `dynamicTypeSize` is an accessibility
    size (`ViewThatFits`).
16. **Sign-up form**: both PIN inputs draw a highlighted first cell at once although only one can have focus; nothing is
    focused on arrival — auto-focus *Your name*.
17. **Planner rows**: the 20 pt genre glyph is read by VoiceOver as just "Jazz" — label it *favourite genre: Jazz*.
18. **Phone width (secondary)**: flag capsules wrap inside themselves ("Due a / group"), activity titles take two lines,
    and the four-segment comfort picker will truncate. The `ViewThatFits` fallback from 15 covers this too.
19. **Portrait framing**: Harold T.'s stock portrait crops close to the head at the circle's top edge — worth a head-room
    check across the 12 bundled portraits.

### Verified fine on iPad
Profile "Group activities" card (participation line, comfort picker, chime); "Group programme" card on both homes;
group check-in steps and *Skip* semantics; Care at home home screen, insights, sign-up; text scaling on the profile at
accessibility-medium (layout holds, nothing clipped).
