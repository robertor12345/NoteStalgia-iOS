# Screen sweep — 30 Oct 2026

A pass over every screen the scripted walkthroughs reach, on the iPad (all eleven iPad stories, scrolled to the end of every
page) and the iPhone (request flow and the home admin dashboard; see "Not covered"). Method: the walkthroughs run fast with
`TEST_RUNNER_DEMO_AUDIT_DIR` set (see `tools/demo/README.md`, "Screen audit"), which saves a screenshot and a control /
text inventory for every step. The inventory is checked for touch targets under 44 pt, missing or repeated labels, overlapping
controls, controls off the edge and likely truncation; the screenshots for text contrast (the 30th-percentile colour behind
each text box against the colour of the text, WCAG 2.x) and by eye. The first pass measured the app as it was after the
"cards at 50 %" change; the last pass is the app after the fixes below.

## Result (iPad, all eleven stories, 12,991 text boxes in the final pass)

| Measure | First pass | Final build |
|---|---|---|
| Text below 3:1 (large) / 4.5:1 (small) | 771 | 381 |
| Text between 3:1 and 4.5:1 | 3,174 | 975 |
| Controls under 44 pt | 603 | 77 |
| Repeated button labels on one screen | 9 | 3 |
| Controls with no label | 0 | 0 |
| Overlapping controls | 54 | 40 |
| Likely truncation | 13 | 12 |

Many of what remains are not defects: text drawn straight on the orb through its shadow halo (the tool cannot see the halo),
controls that overlap by design (a pinned bottom bar over the list that scrolls under it), keyboard accessory buttons and
system sheet grabbers (not ours), and the trend charts that scroll sideways on a phone.

## Fixed

**Correctness**
- Back and Log out were announced by VoiceOver as "NoteStalgia" on every screen (the app's root accessibility label overrode
  them). The label now belongs to the launch splash alone.
- A search that matched nobody showed an empty card; it now says so and what to try.
- The signed-in start screen had a Log out button in the middle of the page and "One-to-one calm" as its action; it is an
  account card with the action named for where it goes, and Log out is in the top bar.
- Log out on a form with unsent work (new resident, observation, session feedback, group check-in) asked nothing and threw
  the work away; it now asks first (`CenteredScrollScreen(logoutWarning:)`).
- Households' "Mood tags" tile showed a bare "—"; the discovery footnote was cut to one line; the group session page ran edge
  to edge (no column, no side margin); the Previous / Pause / Next labels sat at different heights; the roster's tie order
  (equal waits) was not stable between launches (now A–Z); three straight apostrophes in a curly-apostrophe app.
- Two buttons both read "Added" (planner) and two "Resend" / "Revoke" (invites): they now name the people.

**Touch targets (44 pt in the control's own layout)**
- The age stepper, the system segmented controls (display mode, group comfort, share kind, session length, role, homes,
  PIN re-ask, retention, yes / no), toggles, remove-song chips, "Read full narrative", the export / archive / template links,
  the dismiss button on messages, the search clear buttons, the song sheet's Done and Apple Music link, the planner's Clear and
  Whole-home links, PIN reset's Cancel, and every secondary button.

**Contrast and legibility**
- Pink primary actions: 2.1:1 → 8.9:1 (deep-ink label, opaque fill). Log out: 4.15:1 → 6.2:1.
- Cards at 50 % (owner's request) need a firm base under small text: status chips, rating chips, dense list rows (roster,
  dashboard wings and residents, recent sessions, household insights), inset tiles and the selected segment all carry a layer
  of ink; secondary and tertiary text are a step lighter.
- Errors, notices and confirmations are one component, `BrandMessageTile` (icon + words on a firm tile), instead of salmon
  text on the orb; the group warning and the photo-load error moved onto tiles; the planner's resident flags use the shared
  status chip (icon + word) instead of hand-drawn tinted pills.
- Text under 12 pt raised; chart axis labels scale with the text size; tile grids stack at accessibility text sizes; fades
  honour Reduce Motion; cards go opaque with Reduce Transparency.

## Found and not fixed

- Discovery (`testStoryPartB`) failed twice in the sweep at "Clip 1 did not start" on the fast schedule and passed when run
  alone; the clips and the sleeves stream from the network, so this is timing, not layout. Worth watching in the next recording.
- Roughly 380 text boxes are still below the 3:1 / 4.5:1 target, almost all small captions that sit directly on a card over the
  orb's brightest glow. Raising `BrandTheme.cardOpacity` from 0.5 to about 0.6 clears most of them; that is the owner's call
  because it changes the look.
- The short-lived keypad popover on the iPad (a number pad shown as a popover over the form, not docked) appears only when the
  simulator window is open; real iPads dock it.

## Not covered

- iPhone: the request flow and the home admin dashboard were captured and reviewed at phone width. The set-up pages, the
  colleague join and the team page were not captured (the machine ran out of memory mid-run and the longest phone story was
  stopped); the set-up wizard was reviewed on the iPad, and its pages were designed for the phone first, so they are the
  next thing to run: `tools/demo/record-v7.sh` documents the six phone takes, or run `testMobileB`…`testMobileF` with
  `TEST_RUNNER_DEMO_AUDIT_DIR` set.
- Accessibility text sizes: only the roster and the profile were checked at `accessibility-extra-large`.
- VoiceOver: labels and traits were read from the inventory, not heard.
- Screens the scripts do not reach: the camera, the photo library picker, the system share sheet, the PIN-reset flow beyond its
  first step, the Care at home sign-up validation messages, and any screen shown only when a network call fails.

# Round 2 — the new orb, palette and footers (30 Oct 2026, evening)

The orb moved to the GPU and the canvas to indigo (`#4A4488 → #32326E`), so the contrast pass was run again over the same
stories on the iPad (820 pt wide), then the areas that changed.

## Result

323 screens, 8,783 text boxes measured from pixels: **132 below 3:1 (1.5 %) and 114 between 3:1 and 4.5:1 (1.3 %)**.
The earlier pass had roughly 380 below its 3:1 / 4.5:1 target on the old look. The lowest readings (1.05–1.5:1) were spot-checked and are
measurement artefacts, not text a person cannot read: rows scrolled under the old opaque planner footer, titles scrolled
under the top fade ("Sessions in the last 14 days" at y = 67 pt), and screens captured mid-fade ("Preparing the roster…").
The footer slab that caused the planner ones is gone (below). No body text in a mid-page card was found under 4.5:1.

The legibility rule that holds this together is the orb's luminance ceiling per page (`OrbShellConfiguration.interiorBrightness`,
see README "Orb tone per page"): light text over a 50 % card needs what is behind the card at about 0.23 relative luminance or
less. The soft white edge added later (`edgeK` in the shader) is scaled by the same tier, so text pages get a faint edge and the
orb-only pages (resident, immersive) the full one.

## Fixed in this round

- **Pinned bottom bars are a fade, not a slab.** `BrandBottomBarFade` / `.brandBottomBarSurface()`: a progressive blur and an ink
  tint that ramp up from nothing, 30 pt of dissolve above the bar's content; the planner's Start, the set-up wizard's step bar
  and the activity log's Save use it.
- **The orb no longer jumps on resume.** It used to read the wall clock, and it paused for scroll, keyboard and backgrounding, so
  after a 2 s pause the swirl and the breath were a third of a cycle from the last frame. `OrbClock` advances only while an orb
  draws; the GPU orb also stopped pausing for scroll and the keyboard (flag `-NoteStalgiaOrbPauseOnInteraction YES` restores it).
  Scrolling the roster with and without the pause measured the same in a Debug build on the simulator (≈ 45 fps, 14–20 hitches);
  real-device Release numbers are still to take.
- **A dark hairline at the orb's edge** (the corona was attenuated twice across the anti-aliased pixels) is gone, which the soft
  white edge would otherwise have made obvious.
- **Feedback is consistent** (below).
- **Sun and cloud** are flat shapes (`ComfortGlyphs.swift`) in the same colours, used on the resident buttons and in the
  "Enjoyed" / "Moved on from" lists; the tap pulse is the equaliser's wave burst, smaller.

## Sounds and haptics — what is there now

| Moment | Cue |
|---|---|
| Any press, choice, toggle, chip, row | quiet chime + light selection tick (`buttonPress`, 80 ms dedupe) |
| A save, a sign-in, a confirmation | soft impact + rising success chime; the message is read out for VoiceOver |
| An error, a form that cannot be saved | warning haptic + the lower "attention" chime (a minor third) + the message read out |
| Discovery pick, like / skip, playlist start, session settle | soft impact (settle adds the success chime) |

Errors and confirmations on `BrandMessageTile` (sign-in, request, code, Care at home, song search, set-up banner) and the activity
log's failed save carry the cue themselves. iPads have no haptic motor, so the haptics are for the phone pages. Sounds and
haptics are two switches on the roster and the Care at home page ("Sounds and haptics", `FeedbackPreferencesCard`), per device and
no longer tied to Reduce Motion (they used to go silent with it). Verified from the app's own event log in a UI run: each
press logs one chime and one selection haptic, validation failures log the attention pair, saves log impact + success.

## Still open

- Real-device GPU cost of the orb, the wave equaliser and the tap sprite, and the haptics themselves, which the simulator cannot feel.
- Phone width (iPhone 17 Pro) was captured for the roster card, the Sounds and haptics card, the activity log (empty, missing
  lines, noted, correcting, removing), the Care at home form and its "Outside the app" insights card, and the admin programme tiles, and read well; the set-up wizard's own pages at phone width
  were not re-captured.
- The v7 demo films still show the old orb and palette.

# Round 3 — everything again, after one-to-one activities (31 Oct 2026)

A one-resident activity became a one-to-one visit (profile "One-to-one activities" card, planner, handover line), then the whole
app was walked again on the final build.

## What was covered

| Where | What ran | Result |
|---|---|---|
| iPad, default text | all 14 walkthroughs: sign-in to summary, discovery and group, admin, Care at home, planner, activity log (roster, profile, Care at home), group programme, group session, live backend, online discovery, onboarding | 14 / 14 pass (see "Flakes" for the five that needed a second run) |
| iPhone 17 Pro | the six set-up and admin stories (request → set-up → join → dashboard → team), the three activity-log flows, discovery and group | all pass |
| iPad, accessibility-extra-large text | admin, Care at home activity log, discovery and group, plus the roster by hand | layouts reflow (stat tiles stack, the view switch becomes a list); two defects found and fixed |
| Measured from pixels | 142 iPad screens (3,139 text boxes), 80 phone set-up screens (1,549) | no body text on a mid-page card below 4.5:1; the low readings are stat tiles, PIN boxes, system keyboard keys and text scrolled under the edge fades |

## Fixed in this round

- **Tap areas of Previous / Pause / Next overlapped by about a quarter.** The orb glow behind Pause was inside its layout, widening it to
  ~128 pt; it is now a background (drawn, no layout space), so the three buttons are the same width.
- **Response chips read twice to VoiceOver** ("Albert L., Enjoyed it" for the row and for the chip). A chip now says what pressing it does
  ("Enjoyed it for Albert L.").
- **Loose footnotes on the Care at home sign-up and registration pages** sat on the orb in small grey text; they use the on-orb text style.
- **Trend charts at accessibility sizes:** the five y-axis numbers of the 54 pt tiles stacked on top of each other; the small charts keep the
  lowest and highest, the expanded chart keeps all.
- **Category chips at accessibility sizes:** the icon ran into its label; the icon slot now scales with the text.
- **The group session's Pause button no longer reaches into its neighbours** (above) and the planner's footer fade is verified at phone width.

## Flakes, and what they were

Not app defects, but they cost reruns, so they are written down:

- **The first test after a simulator boots fails on its first keystroke** ("neither element nor any descendant has keyboard focus"). The same
  typing works from the second test on. Run any typing test once before a recording.
- **A tap right after a scroll lands on a page that is still coasting** and only stops it ("Activity log did not appear", "Empty insights did
  not appear"). The test helper `scrollTo` now lets the page settle, and nudges a control that came to rest under the bottom fade into the
  clear, before the caller taps.
- Mobile B's clear-and-retype step lost keyboard focus once (a UI-test quirk on the phone); it passed on the rerun (18 min at full pace).
- The resident-surface long-press and the planner list are not reachable by the iPad-shaped tests at phone width (the observation form and the
  list are longer than a screen); I checked by hand that the long-press opens the observation form on the phone.

## Not covered

- Roster-driven stories at accessibility-extra-large text: the tests cannot scroll far enough through the page to find a resident.
- VoiceOver was read from the inventory, not heard; haptics cannot be felt in the simulator; real-device GPU cost is unmeasured.
