# UX / UI review — 28 Oct 2026 (round 6: every page)

Scope: every phase in `FlowRootView` (31 screens), with the brief to put **Log out top-right**, raise the
**colour / contrast / borders / separation of every handover note and data point**, and lift
**accessibility** to the usual standards (WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, 44 pt targets, headers, values, Dynamic
Type, no colour-only meaning). Method: code read of every screen file, a walk on **iPad Air 11** (primary
target) against the deployed Worker, contrast arithmetic on the brand palette, and the fixes applied in the
same pass. Items from the 26 Oct review that were still open are picked up where they fit.

---

## A. What changed everywhere (shared)

| Change | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Log out moved to the top-right** of the staff chrome; Back stays top-left | `FlowTopStaffNavBar` (every `CenteredScrollScreen`, discovery), the group session's own bar | Industry pattern: navigation leads, the account action trails, and the two are no longer a 8 pt mis-tap apart |
| **Data display tokens** on `BrandTheme`: `cardBorder`, `surfaceInset`, `insetBorder`, `hairline`, `meterTrack`, `statusPositive / Neutral / Caution / Attention` | `Core/BrandTheme.swift` | One three-step surface grammar (card → inset tile → hairline rows) instead of ad-hoc `creamDeep.opacity(0.75)` tiles and `white.opacity(0.08)` tracks that measured 1.3:1 against the card |
| **Shared components** `BrandSectionHeader`, `BrandInsetTile`, `BrandStatTile`, `BrandMeterBar`, `BrandRatingRow`, `BrandRatingTone`, `BrandStatusChip`, `BrandHairline`, `BrandQuoteBlock`, `BrandKeyValueRow`, `BrandSegmentedChoice`, `BrandSelectableChip` | `Core/BrandDataDisplay.swift` (new) | Every number, rating, chip and quoted note now renders the same way, reads to VoiceOver the same way, and the status tints are explained by a legend wherever they are used |
| Card border 0.28 → 0.34 | `BrandCard` | Cards separate from the orb behind them |
| Section titles are **headers** (`.isHeader`) | every card | VoiceOver users jump section to section |
| Meters read their **value** ("Mood, 7 out of 10"); progress capsules read "Step 2 of 4" | all rating rows, step progress | Previously the bars were silent and the steps were a plain label |
| Trend / status is **icon + word**, never a colour dot | admin dashboard rows, household header, planner chips | WCAG 1.4.1 (use of colour) |
| Segmented controls fall back to a **vertical option list at accessibility text sizes** | share kind, roster display mode (unchanged picker, see C), group comfort, session length, household relationship | The system segmented control never scales its labels (26 Oct B15) |
| Unselected chips: border `insetBorder` (was gold 0.22); selected chips carry a ✓ | context tags, environment tags | Selected state no longer colour-only; unselected chips visible on the card |

Contrast of the status tints as text on the card surface (`cream` 0.16/0.15/0.24): positive 10:1,
caution 11:1, attention 7.9:1, neutral 9:1, `textTertiary` 6.3:1. Meter track 0.22 white ≈ 2.1:1 against
the card (decorative; the fill against the track is ≥ 3:1 for every tint).

## Walk-through findings fixed on the spot (iPad Air 11, deployed Worker)

- Tiles in a row were uneven when a label wrapped ("Emotional presentation"): `BrandStatTile` now fills its row
  and `BrandStatTileRow` sizes the row to the tallest tile (a bare `HStack` of filling tiles soaked up spare
  height in the Care at home card — caught and fixed in the same pass).
- Context chips truncated ("Agitated / beh…"): the fixed-column grid behind them is now the content-sized
  wrapping flow, so every tag reads in full.
- Session history repeated the interaction summary twice (as a line and again inside the insight tile); the
  line now only appears for sessions without an insight, and "Mood tags: —" is hidden.
- The dashboard's third trend chart still clipped with four series: on iPad the charts are a grid (three across,
  or two by two), the horizontal scroll stays for phone width.
- Recent-session rows read "Residential · —" for sessions without mood tags; the dash is dropped.
- Planner copy "this fortnight" → "in 14 days"; Care at home line reads "Claire · family member · cared for at home".
- Group session transport labels (Previous / Pause / Next) sat on the orb without a halo; they take the overlay text style.

5. **Per-resident group check-in (B12)** — the last check-in step adds **Who stood out?**: one chip per
   participant cycling engaged → unsettled → left early → clear. Stored as `participantNotes` on the record

## E. Organisation set-up wizard (round 7)

Findings and fixes are listed in `docs/HANDOFF_PLAN.md` ("Set-up wizard UX review"). Principles carried into the
house style: one primary action per screen; no two controls share a label; the top-left control leaves a flow and
says where, the bottom bar moves within it; unsent work is asked about, never dropped; feedback appears next to
the control that raised it and is announced; equal margins inside cards (dividers between rows only); admin and
data pages are phone-first, resident and staff-session pages iPad-first.

## F. Paged wizard and form validation (round 8)

Principles: a form page fits a phone screen without scrolling where it can, so a required field is never below
the fold; one journey per page group (add a home, invite someone) with its own commit button, returning to the
list it started from; a field and its button stack on a phone and sit side by side on the iPad; every required
field is named in a message under the field (icon + words, never colour alone) and the banner counts several; a
field only filters characters that can never be right (digits, spaces in an email, `<` `>`), everything else is
checked and explained. The rule set lives once per side (`InputRules.swift`, `validate.ts`) and is documented in
the README catalogue ("Input validation"). Open: weak PINs, a home's own address, a language other than English.
